DATA & PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

Smart Display is a personal Raspberry Pi dashboard operated by its owner. This policy explains how it accesses, uses, stores, and shares information from an authorized Google account.

What Google information is accessed

With authorization, the app requests the Gmail read-only scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly). It can read email messages and metadata, including subjects, senders, dates, and message contents. This permission does not allow the app to send, edit, or delete email.

Why it is accessed

The app uses the authorized Gmail information to identify and show relevant school email notifications, such as course announcements and assignment-related messages, on the owner's private display. School-related information may also be included when the owner requests an AI-assisted answer or in a scheduled briefing.

Where it is processed and stored

OAuth credentials are stored on the owner's private Raspberry Pi, not on this public website. Gmail messages are retrieved from Google's API and processed for dashboard alerts. Some information may be cached temporarily for the display. The original messages stay in the user's Google mailbox.

When the owner enables relevant AI features, selected information from messages may be sent to OpenAI to generate a response or spoken briefing. This is distinct from hosting this informational website. The private dashboard is not publicly accessible through this website.

Sharing and advertising

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. The app uses information only for the owner's authorized personal features. Google and optional AI service providers process information as needed to deliver those features.

Removing access

The owner can revoke Google's authorization at Google Account connections. The owner can also delete saved authorization tokens and other locally stored data from the Raspberry Pi.

Public website

This information page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not request access to visitor Google accounts and does not deliberately collect email contents. GitHub may collect standard hosting logs under its own privacy policies.

Updates and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data handling change. For questions, reach the maintainer through the GitHub project repository.

Note: This policy describes the intended current operation of Smart Display. Review it against the running implementation before submitting it for Google OAuth verification.
